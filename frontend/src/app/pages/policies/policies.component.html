Last Updated: November 2026

1. 20 km Straight-Line Delivery Radius

We provide express local delivery to customer addresses located within a strict 20-kilometer straight-line boundary (calculated using high-precision Haversine great-circle algorithms).

2. Dispatch Windows & Timings

Orders are dispatched daily during active store operational hours:

  • Monday – Saturday: 09:30 AM to 08:00 PM

  • Orders placed within scheduled slot cut-off buffers are dispatched on the same day.

Orders eligible for local delivery qualify for FREE Delivery when the order subtotal meets or exceeds our promotional free-delivery threshold (currently ₹499.00). Standard nominal courier fees apply to orders below this threshold.

4. Order Verification & Delivery Confirmation

For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, customer phone verification via a one-time passcode (OTP) or phone confirmation may be required to protect against fake or mistaken bookings before our dispatch team departs.

🔄 Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Product-Specific Return Eligibility

At NOVA PETZLIFE, pet wellness and hygiene are paramount. Return eligibility is governed on a per-product basis:

  • Eligible Products: Sealed, unopened pet food bags, pet bowls, carriers, leashes, harnesses, and non-hygiene accessories are eligible for return within 7 days of delivery.
  • Non-Returnable Products: Opened wet food cans/pouches, opened kibble, pet cosmetics, hygiene/litter products, chew toys that have come into contact with saliva, and perishable veterinary supplements are strictly non-returnable to prevent cross-contamination.

2. How to Request a Return

Customers can initiate a return through their account dashboard or by visiting our Support page. Please provide the order number, item details, and clear photographs of the intact seal and packaging.

3. Inspection & Refund Timeline

Upon return pickup or in-store drop-off, our staff inspects the returned merchandise. Once approved, refunds are processed:

  • Prepaid Orders (Razorpay): Reimbursed directly to the original bank account, UPI, or card within 5–7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Processed via verified direct UPI transfer or store credit upon customer confirmation.
